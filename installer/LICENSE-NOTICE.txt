Qumaxa AR Automation Connector — notice before installation

This program installs a small background service on this computer. It
connects outbound, over HTTPS only, to the Qumaxa AR Automation Console
(console.qumaxa.com). Nothing on this computer listens for incoming
connections.

What it does
  - Sends a heartbeat so the Console knows the connector is alive and
    which version is installed.
  - For Microsoft Dynamics GP: reads open invoices, customers, payments and
    credit limits from your GP company database (read-only, SQL Server). It
    never writes to GP.
  - For QuickBooks Online: nothing on this computer talks to QuickBooks. You
    connect your company from the Console portal through Intuit's own
    consent screen.
  - Optionally, sends and reads email through your own mail server for
    invoice delivery, reminders and inbox monitoring.

What stays on this computer
  - Your mailbox and SQL Server credentials (if you provide them) are stored
    only on this machine, encrypted with Windows data protection, in
    %ProgramData%\Qumaxa\ARConnector. They are never sent to the Console.
  - The Console never asks for, and never receives, your accounting system
    passwords.

Why we ask for your exact software version
  Support quality depends on it. Knowing the precise version lets us
  reproduce and fix a problem instead of guessing.

Uninstalling
  Use "Apps & features" or the Start Menu shortcut. The service is removed;
  you choose whether the stored configuration is deleted too.

By continuing you confirm you have read this notice. The installer records
that it was shown (acknowledged_security_notice) for your records and ours.
