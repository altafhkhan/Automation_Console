AR Automation Console

Your company's own approval queue — only your team can see or act on this
Signed in as mina.rahimi@harborpine.example · Controller · Harbor & Pine Supplies Ltd.

Every action with money or customer risk attached stops here until a named person approves it. The agents only propose; your policy (approver + threshold per action type) decides who signs off.

The connector reads your AR mailbox. Today every message is handed to a human in draft-only mode: you write or edit the reply here, and the connector sends it through your own mail server. Messages with legal or angry wording are flagged for escalation. Automatic classification and AI-drafted replies are on the roadmap.

Everything the AR agent and its sub-agents did in the last cycle, with the outcome: completed, needs approval, or exception (parked for a human).

Every decision — by an agent or by a person — is written to the audit log with who, what and the full detail. Your own approvals on this page appear here too.

All figures are computed deterministically from open invoices and cases — an AI model never calculates a number. Metrics that need sales history (DSO, CEI) are shown as unavailable until that data source is connected.

Exposure versus credit limit per customer, pulled from the accounting system. Limit changes are requests: they go to the approval queue, and a human applies them.

One installation per accounting system. QuickBooks Online connects through Intuit's own consent screen; Dynamics GP uses the installed connector with read-only SQL access. Subscriptions are handled by Stripe Checkout.

The daily summary the Console produces for your team: what ran, what is waiting on you, and what needs attention.
