Only classified, non-escalated messages appear here. Escalated or unclassifiable messages need a human to handle them directly (see the Tasks tab) — this isn't a queue for those.

Record of completed tenant deletions — proof an erasure request actually erased something, since the tenant row itself no longer exists to check.

Credit limits here are set manually, not pulled from an ERP — QuickBooks Online has no native credit-limit field (unlike QuickBooks Desktop), so this is the honest way to record one against a real customer. Blank means Credit Risk has no known limit for that customer and will keep skipping it rather than guessing.

One row per installation per day, one column per sub-agent — a real count of ar_tasks, not a separate metric that could drift from what actually happened. Hover a number for its status breakdown (completed, needs approval, exception, pending).
